What Vallik is

Vallik is a free app that lets a group of skiers see where each person is, work out routes over pistes and lifts and meet up at the resort. By using it you accept these terms and the privacy policy.

The mountain comes first

  • Maps, routes, times, lift hours, slope gradients and alerts are for guidance only. They may be out of date or contain mistakes.
  • Always follow the signs, the official status of pistes and lifts and the instructions of resort staff.
  • Choose routes that match your level and the conditions of the day. Vallik doesn't recommend leaving marked pistes.
  • In an emergency, call 112. The "I need help" alert in the app only tells your group and depends on signal.

Peter

Peter is an AI assistant. Its answers can be wrong or out of date. Check before making important decisions, especially about safety.
