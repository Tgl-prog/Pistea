Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short: Vallik uses your name and your position at the resort so the people in your group can see you on the map. Only your group sees them, we don't sell data or show ads, and you can delete everything whenever you want.

Who is responsible

Vallik is an app for groups of skiers, currently available in Les 3 Vallées. For any question about your data, write to vallik-soporte@googlegroups.com.

What data we use and why

DataPurpose
Your nameSo your group knows who you are on the map and in messages.
Your profile photo, if you choose to add oneSo your group recognises you on the map. It is stored at a small size (about 160 px) and only your group sees it. You can remove it at any time from your profile.
Your Google or Apple account (name and email), if you choose to sign in with itTo get your profile back on another phone. If you join with just your name, we don't use any account.
Your positionTo show you on the map to your group and to work out routes, the way back home and the meeting point. It is saved when you press "I'm here" or while "Live" is on, never without you turning it on.
Your position while voice guidance is onIf you turn on voice guidance on a route, the app reads your GPS only on your phone to tell you each step. It is not saved or sent to anyone, unless "Live" is also on.
Your route of the dayWhile "Live" is on, the app keeps your route only on your phone so you can replay it in "My day". It is not sent to anyone and is deleted automatically after about 15 days.
Pistes you log in your dayYour summary of the day and your group's.
Messages and alerts"I'm waiting here", "I need help", meeting points and alerts about pistes or lifts that you share with your group.
Questions to PeterTo answer you. See the section about Peter.
Data stored on your phoneYour name, the group code, your language, where you're staying, your route and a copy of the map, so the app works without signal. It doesn't leave your phone.

The legal basis is your consent, which you give when you join a group and turn on location, and providing the service you ask for. You can withdraw it at any time by stopping sharing your position or leaving the group.

Who sees your data

Your name, photo, position, messages and pistes are only seen by the people in your group, that is, whoever has its link or code. There are no public profiles, we don't sell data and we don't use it for advertising.

Peter, the AI guide

When you ask Peter something, we send your question with minimal context: the resort, the time, your language, the name of the piste or place where you are, nearby restaurants and bars, what is closed and your group's alerts. We don't send the names or photos of the people in your group. The question goes through our server and is answered by an Anthropic model (Claude), which doesn't use these conversations to train its models.

Providers we use

  • Google Firebase: sign-in and the group database.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app's website.
  • Vercel and Anthropic: only for Peter.
  • Google and Apple: only if you choose to sign in with their account.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. They do so with the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • When you press Leave the group, your position, your profile (including your photo) and your messages in that group are deleted.
  • If the group's creator removes you, they are deleted in the same way.
  • Everything is also deleted automatically: your position and messages after 2 days; alerts and the meeting point one day after they expire; your profile 60 days after you last opened the group; and each logged piste 60 days after you logged it.
  • With Delete my account, in your profile inside the app, your profile, photo, position, messages, alerts and logged pistes in your current group are deleted, along with your account. If you were in other groups before, write to us and we will delete that too within 30 days at most.
  • Data stored on your phone, including your route, is deleted when you delete the app or your browser data.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict, take away or object to the use of your data by writing to vallik-soporte@googlegroups.com. If you think we are not handling it properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to the data protection authority in your country.

Children

Vallik is not meant for children under 14 in Spain, or under 15 in France. If you are under that age, only use the app with permission from your parents or guardians.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, if the change is important, let you know inside the app.
